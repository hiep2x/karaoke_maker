About Karaoke Maker

Karaoke Maker is designed to simplify the karaoke creation process, from audio processing and lyric alignment to video customization and export.

With Karaoke Maker, you can import a song, separate vocals from the instrumental track, adjust the song key, add lyrics, align lyrics with the music, and customize the visual style of your karaoke video. Once everything is ready, you can export the finished video for saving or sharing on other platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about using Karaoke Maker.

What is Karaoke Maker used for?

Karaoke Maker helps you create karaoke videos from songs by preparing an instrumental track, adding lyrics, aligning lyric timing, and customizing the final video design.

Can I change the key of a song?

Yes. Karaoke Maker allows you to adjust the song key so you can choose a pitch that feels more comfortable for your voice.

Can I customize how the lyrics look?

Yes. You can customize fonts, text size, colors, backgrounds, and other visual settings for your karaoke video.
